# Impediment Management

**Escalation rule:** any blocker older than 5 business days, or threatening a Sprint Goal/release, receives a recovery date and escalation path.

| ID | Impediment | Category | Age | Owner | Resolution | Status | Recurrence Control |
|---|---|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| IMP-001 | Urgent customer escalations | Process | 5 | Product Owner / Scrum Master | Clarified change-entry rule; routed noncritical work to refinement. | Closed | No recurrence in final 3 sprints |
| IMP-002 | Backlog readiness gaps | Quality | 7 | QA / Engineering Lead | Moved validation earlier and added test-readiness review. | Closed | Validation completed before final sprint day |
| IMP-003 | Support engineering interruption | Dependency | 9 | Dependency Owner | Added twice-weekly dependency sync and acceptance date. | Closed | Aging reduced below 4 days |
| IMP-004 | Release coordination | Capacity | 4 | Delivery Lead | Protected critical capacity and rebalanced lower-priority work. | Closed | No release-critical conflict |
| IMP-005 | Acceptance-criteria ambiguity | Product | 6 | Product Owner | Applied priority policy and explicit tradeoff for late changes. | Closed | Sprint churn reduced |
| IMP-006 | Ambiguous acceptance criteria | Backlog | 3 | Product Owner | Added refinement checklist and examples. | Closed | Ready rate reached 94% |
| IMP-007 | Work aging without visible owner | Flow | 8 | Scrum Master | Created aging review and swarm trigger. | Closed | Oldest active item reduced to 5 days |
| IMP-008 | Retro actions repeatedly deferred | Team | 5 | Scrum Team | Moved improvement actions into visible backlog with owners. | Closed | Action completion reached final KPI |

The Scrum Master makes impediments visible, helps identify the correct owner, removes organizational friction and escalates only when the team lacks authority.
