# Continuous Improvement Report

## Executive Summary
Across six sprints, the delivery system moved from unstable commitments and late blocker discovery toward stronger backlog readiness, lower WIP, faster impediment resolution, measurable retrospective experiments, healthier ceremonies and clearer Sprint Goals.

## Results

| KPI | Baseline | Final |
|---|---:|---:|
| Velocity | 44 | **72** |
| Carryover | 36% | **10%** |
| Escaped defects | 12 | **3** |
| Stakeholder satisfaction | 6.6/10 | **9.0/10** |
| Release predictability | 52% | **88%** |
| Retro action completion | 37% | **87%** |
| Median cycle time | 9.8 days | **5.5 days** |
| Blocked-work rate | 22% | **8%** |

## Strongest Interventions
1. Definition of Ready reduced false starts.
2. Shift-left quality reduced late validation.
3. Impediment aging surfaced blockers earlier.
4. Retrospective experiments improved follow-through.
5. WIP awareness and swarming reduced cycle time.
6. Coaching reduced Scrum Master dependency.
7. Sprint Goal language improved stakeholder conversations.

## Sustainability Controls
- Review working agreements every two retrospectives.
- Keep experiments visible until validated.
- Maintain dependency and blocker aging thresholds.
- Run quarterly maturity and team-health checks.
- Protect metrics from individual-ranking misuse.
