# Working Agreements

1. Sprint Goal first; new work enters an active sprint only after an explicit tradeoff.
2. Raise blockers within one working day.
3. No silent disagreement; respectful challenge is expected.
4. Important actions have an owner and due date.
5. Quality is shared and starts before the final sprint days.
6. Respect focus time and avoid unnecessary meetings.
7. Work that does not meet Definition of Ready is not forecast.
8. Limit WIP and finish before starting more work when practical.
9. Ask for help early; pairing and swarming are encouraged.
10. Retrospective actions are visible backlog items with success measures.
11. Use blameless root-cause analysis.
12. Material decisions are documented.

## Conflict Protocol
Clarify facts, let affected people explain concerns, separate preferences from constraints, identify options, decide at the lowest responsible level, and escalate only when authority or risk requires it.

## Review Cadence
Review every second retrospective and after major team-composition changes.
