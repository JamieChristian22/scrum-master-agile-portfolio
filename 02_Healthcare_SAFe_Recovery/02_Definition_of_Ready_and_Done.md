# Definition of Ready and Definition of Done

## Definition of Ready
A backlog item is ready when:
- user or business outcome is clear;
- acceptance criteria are testable;
- dependencies have owners;
- required designs, data, access and environments are understood;
- security/compliance needs are identified;
- item is small enough for the sprint;
- Developers have discussed and estimated it;
- Product Owner priority is clear;
- no unresolved question blocks implementation;
- relevant nonfunctional expectations are documented.

**Ready threshold:** 9 of 10 criteria plus explicit acceptance of any exception.

## Definition of Done
Done requires:
- acceptance criteria pass;
- peer review complete;
- required automated/manual tests pass;
- no open critical defect;
- security/privacy checks complete where applicable;
- documentation/runbook changes complete;
- monitoring/logging updated where applicable;
- Product Owner acceptance recorded;
- work integrated into the approved environment;
- operational-support impact addressed;
- evidence attached;
- item potentially releasable.
