# Scrum Master Coaching Plan

| Audience | Observed Need | Coaching Approach | Practice | Success Signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Product Owner | Priority changes disrupt focus | Decision framing | Priority policy + refinement prep | Fewer mid-sprint changes |
| Product Owner | Stories arrive unclear | Question-based coaching | Outcome + acceptance checklist | Ready rate >=90% |
| Developers | Daily Scrum is status reporting | Facilitation coaching | Team-to-team discussion | Team self-facilitates |
| Developers | Too much work starts | Flow coaching | WIP review + swarm trigger | Lower cycle time |
| QA / Engineering | Quality happens late | Cross-functional coaching | Earlier test discussion | Fewer late defects |
| Team | Retro actions are forgotten | Improvement coaching | 2-action cap + experiment tracker | >=85% completion |
| Stakeholders | Output-heavy expectations | Agile education | Sprint Goal / forecast language | Fewer task escalations |
| Leadership | Metrics risk misuse | Systems coaching | Explain metric intent | No individual ranking |

The Scrum Master coaches the system and behaviors rather than acting as a task manager.
