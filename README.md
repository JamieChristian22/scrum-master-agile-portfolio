# Scrum Master & Agile Delivery Portfolio

## Enterprise Agile Facilitation • Coaching • Flow • Continuous Improvement • SAFe • DevOps

**Jamie Christian II**

This portfolio contains six fully developed Agile delivery simulations designed to demonstrate Scrum Master capability beyond ceremony facilitation. It shows how a Scrum Master improves team systems through coaching, impediment removal, Sprint Goal discipline, flow management, retrospective experiments, dependency management, team-health measurement, and transparent stakeholder communication.

> **Disclosure:** All projects are realistic portfolio simulations. Organizations, team sizes, metrics, outcomes, and operational scenarios are fictionalized for demonstration and are not presented as employment history.

## Scrum Master Capabilities Demonstrated

- Scrum facilitation and self-management
- Sprint planning, capacity and Sprint Goal discipline
- Backlog refinement and Definition of Ready
- Definition of Done and quality discipline
- Impediment aging, escalation, root cause and recurrence control
- Retrospective experiments with measurable hypotheses
- Team health and psychological-safety signals
- Flow metrics: cycle time, throughput, WIP, blocked time and aging work
- Cross-team dependency management
- Product Owner and delivery-team coaching
- Agile maturity assessment
- Ceremony effectiveness measurement
- SAFe / ART coordination where appropriate
- DevOps collaboration and operational delivery
- Release readiness and stakeholder communication
