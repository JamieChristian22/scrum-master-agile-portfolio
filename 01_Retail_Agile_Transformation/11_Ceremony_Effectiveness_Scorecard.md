# Ceremony Effectiveness Scorecard

| Ceremony | Baseline | Final | Main Problem | Facilitation Change |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Daily Scrum | 6.1 | 8.9 | Status reporting | Goal/blocker/plan prompts |
| Sprint Planning | 6.5 | 9.0 | Overcommitment | Historical capacity + Sprint Goal |
| Backlog Refinement | 5.8 | 9.1 | Unready stories | DoR + dependency scan |
| Sprint Review | 7.0 | 9.3 | Demo-heavy | Outcome / feedback framing |
| Retrospective | 5.9 | 9.4 | Actions not completed | Experiment tracker |
| Cross-Team Sync | 5.7 | 8.8 | Late dependencies | Aging review + owners |

A ceremony that remains below 7/10 is inspected for purpose, invitees, preparation, prompts and redundancy rather than automatically extended.
